# Assemble Sentence Task Prompt

You are assembling word-level ASR output into proper sentences with timestamps.

## Context
The following text contains word-by-word transcription with timestamps. Your task is to combine these words into complete sentences while preserving timing information.

## Guidelines
1. Combine words that form a complete sentence
2. Use the start time of the first word and end time of the last word
3. Add appropriate punctuation based on context
4. Handle repeated/duplicate words appropriately
5. Preserve the natural flow of speech

## Output Format
For each assembled sentence, output:
{sentence_start_time} {sentence_end_time} "{complete_sentence}"

Example:
166.64 169.84 "あ、音来てますか?"
