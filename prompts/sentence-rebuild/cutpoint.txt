# Cutpoint Task Prompt

You are analyzing word-level ASR output to find optimal sentence boundaries (cutpoints) for splitting a long transcription into manageable segments.

## Context
The following text contains approximately {lines_per_segment} lines of word-by-word transcription around a proposed cutpoint. Your task is to find the best place to split the text into two segments.

## Guidelines for Good Cutpoints
1. End of a complete sentence (after punctuation like 。!? etc.)
   - A cutpoint should be the ending of a sentence.
2. Natural pause in speech (indicated by longer time gaps)
3. Topic or speaker change
4. Avoid cutting in the middle of:
   - A word
   - A phrase or idiom
   - A sentence
5. Cutpoint points to the ending of a sentence part,
   cutpoint+1 line starts a new sentence part.

## Your Task
Review the context and suggest the optimal line number for the cutpoint.

Response format:
- CUTPOINT_LINE: [line number]
- REASON: [brief explanation of why this is a good split point]
- CONFIDENCE: [high/medium/low]
